# Time off calendar

The Time off calendar shows who is off on each day. Every Person can open it.
The page is read-only. To change Time off, use the Schedule. See [Schedule view](schedule.md).

## Read it

- The page shows one month as a wall calendar. It opens on the current month.
- Weeks run from Monday to Sunday. The grid shows full weeks.
  Days of the previous and next month are grey. Their Time off still shows.
- Each Time off is a bar with the name of the Person. The bar covers all days of the Time off, also weekends, public holidays and Company days off.
  The day number and the name of a day off stay above the bars.
- Time off that crosses a Sunday continues in the next week. The name repeats there. The bar has a flat edge where it continues.
- A bar that starts in the middle of a day means the Time off starts in the afternoon.
  A bar that ends in the middle of a day means the Time off ends in the morning. A one-day half day fills half of the day.
- Bars that overlap sit under each other, sorted by name. A week row grows taller when many People are off.
- Point at a bar to see the full name when it is cut off.

## Move around

Select the arrows to go to the previous or next month. Select **Today** to return to the current month.
